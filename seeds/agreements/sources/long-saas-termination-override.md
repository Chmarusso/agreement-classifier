## 2. Subscription and access

2.1 Subject to the Company paying the Charges, the Provider grants the Company and its Authorised Users a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Platform and the Documentation for the internal business purposes of the Company and its Affiliates.

2.2 The Company may increase the number of Authorised Users or add modules at any time by written order at the prices in Schedule 2, pro-rated for the remainder of the current subscription year.

2.3 The Company shall not, and shall ensure that Authorised Users do not, resell the Platform, attempt to reverse engineer it except as permitted by Applicable Law, or use it to build a competing product.

2.4 The Provider may suspend access of an individual Authorised User who breaches the acceptable use policy in the Documentation, after notifying the Company and giving it a reasonable opportunity to address the breach.

## 3. Provider obligations

3.1 The Provider shall provide the Platform substantially in accordance with the Documentation and the service levels in Schedule 3, using Good Industry Practice.

3.2 The Provider shall host the Platform and all Company Data in data centres located in the European Union, currently in Frankfurt and Amsterdam, and shall not change the hosting location without the Company's prior written consent.

3.3 The Provider may update the Platform from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the functionality, security or performance of the Platform. The Provider shall give at least thirty (30) days' notice of any change that requires action by the Company.

3.4 The Provider shall provide the support services described in Schedule 4.

## 4. Company obligations

4.1 The Company is responsible for the accuracy and legality of the Company Data it uploads and for the acts and omissions of its Authorised Users.

4.2 The Company shall keep login credentials confidential, enable multi-factor authentication for all Authorised Users, and notify the Provider promptly of any suspected unauthorised use of the Platform.

4.3 The Company shall maintain its own network connectivity and hardware as reasonably required to access the Platform in accordance with the Documentation.

## 5. Charges and payment

5.1 The Company shall pay the subscription fees and any other Charges set out in Schedule 2. Subscription fees are invoiced annually in advance at the start of each subscription year.

5.2 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt. All amounts are exclusive of VAT, which is payable in addition.

5.3 If the Company fails to pay an undisputed invoice by its due date, the Provider may charge statutory interest for late payment in commercial transactions and, after giving thirty (30) days' further written notice, suspend access to the Platform until payment is made.

5.4 The Provider may increase the subscription fees on renewal by giving at least ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the current term, provided that any increase does not exceed five per cent per year.

## 6. Service levels and support

6.1 The Provider shall make the Platform available in accordance with the availability commitment in Schedule 3 and shall pay the service credits set out there if it fails to do so.

6.2 Service credits are the Company's sole financial remedy for failure to meet the availability commitment, save that repeated failure constitutes a material breach as described in Schedule 3.

6.3 Planned maintenance shall take place outside 07:00 to 20:00 Warsaw time on Business Days and shall not exceed eight (8) hours in any calendar month.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not use it for any purpose other than the performance of its obligations or the exercise of its rights under this Agreement.

7.2 Each party may disclose the other party's Confidential Information to its employees, officers, representatives, subcontractors and advisers who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement, provided that it ensures that each such recipient is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this clause.

7.3 A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law, by a court of competent jurisdiction or by any governmental or regulatory authority, provided that, to the extent legally permitted, it gives the other party as much notice of the disclosure as possible.

7.4 The obligations in this clause do not apply to information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement, was lawfully in the possession of the receiving party before disclosure, or is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information.

7.5 On termination or expiry of this Agreement each party shall, at the request of the other, return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information, save that the Provider may retain copies required by Applicable Law, which remain subject to this clause.

7.6 The obligations of confidentiality in this clause continue for a period of five (5) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement, and indefinitely in respect of trade secrets.

9.1 The parties acknowledge that, for the purposes of the Data Protection Legislation, the Company is the Controller and the Provider is the Processor of any Personal Data processed on behalf of the Company under this Agreement.

## 10. Information security

10.1 The Provider shall maintain an information security management system certified to ISO/IEC 27001, or an equivalent standard approved in writing by the Company, for the duration of this Agreement.

10.2 The Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Company Data against accidental, unauthorised or unlawful processing, destruction, loss, damage or disclosure, having regard to the state of the art, the cost of implementation and the risks to Data Subjects.

10.3 The Provider shall ensure that all Company Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent algorithm, and that encryption keys are managed in accordance with Good Industry Practice.

10.4 The Provider shall carry out penetration tests of the systems used to provide the services at least once in each calendar year using an independent tester, and shall share a summary of the results and its remediation plan with the Company within thirty (30) days of receiving the report.

10.5 The Provider shall maintain role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and logs of access to Company Data that are retained for at least twelve (12) months and made available to the Company on request.

14.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months.

## 15. Termination

15.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party at least thirty (30) days' written notice. On termination for convenience by the Company, the Provider shall refund any prepaid subscription fees for the period after the termination date.

15.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.

15.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, enters into restructuring or bankruptcy proceedings, or ceases to carry on business.

## 16. Consequences of termination

16.1 For thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, the Provider shall allow the Company to export all Company Data in a standard machine-readable format at no additional charge.

16.2 After the export period the Provider shall delete all Company Data from the Platform and its backups within ninety (90) days and confirm the deletion in writing.

16.3 Termination does not affect any rights or remedies accrued before termination, and clauses that by their nature survive termination continue in force.

## 17. Continuity of service and escrow

17.1 The Provider shall deposit the source code of the Platform, together with build instructions and deployment scripts, with an independent escrow agent approved by the Company, and shall update the deposit within thirty (30) days of each major release.

17.2 The Company may obtain release of the deposit if the Provider becomes insolvent, ceases to maintain the Platform, or fails to remedy a P1 incident within ten (10) Business Days, and may then use the deposit solely to maintain the Platform for its own internal purposes.

17.3 The Provider shall bear the fees of the escrow agent. The Company may require a verification test of the deposit once in each year at the Provider's cost.

## 18. Business continuity and disaster recovery

18.1 The Provider shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan that enables it to continue providing the services, or to restore them within the recovery time objective of four (4) hours and the recovery point objective of one (1) hour, following a disruptive event.

18.2 The Provider shall test the plan at least once in each calendar year, including a full failover test of production systems, and shall provide the Company with a written summary of the test results, any gaps identified and the actions taken to close them.

18.3 The Provider shall invoke the plan promptly on the occurrence of a disruptive event and shall keep the Company informed of progress at intervals of no more than four (4) hours until the services are fully restored.

18.4 The existence of a force majeure event does not relieve the Provider of its obligation to implement the plan, and the Provider may rely on force majeure only to the extent that a properly implemented plan would not have prevented or mitigated the effect of the event.
