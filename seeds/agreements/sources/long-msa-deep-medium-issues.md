# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement is made on the date of the last signature below between Northwind Analytics sp. z o.o., a limited liability company with its registered office at ul. Prosta 51, 00-838 Warsaw, Poland, entered in the register of entrepreneurs under KRS number 0000712345 (the "Company"), and Orbital Systems Integration S.A., a company incorporated in Luxembourg with its registered office at 12 rue Eugène Ruppert, L-2453 Luxembourg (the "Supplier"), each a "party" and together the "parties".

Background: the Company wishes to modernise its data platform and analytics capabilities, and the Supplier has agreed to provide data engineering and consulting services on the terms of this Agreement. Orbital has represented that it has the expertise, personnel and certifications needed to do so.

1.7 "Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by or on behalf of a party, whether before or after the Effective Date, in any form or medium, including information relating to the disclosing party's business, customers, finances, technology, products, know-how and trade secrets.

1.8 "Data Protection Legislation" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the GDPR), the Polish Act of 10 May 2018 on the protection of personal data, and all other Applicable Law relating to the processing of personal data and privacy.

1.9 "Effective Date" means the date on which this Agreement has been signed by both parties.

1.11 "Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, rights to inventions, copyright and related rights, trade marks, business names, domain names, rights in get-up, goodwill, rights in designs, rights in computer software, database rights, rights to preserve the confidentiality of information and all other intellectual property rights, in each case whether registered or unregistered, in any part of the world.

## 2. Scope and statements of work

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services and the Deliverables to the Company in accordance with this Agreement and each Statement of Work.

2.2 Each Statement of Work forms a separate contract incorporating the terms of this Agreement. In the event of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over the schedules, and the schedules prevail over any Statement of Work, unless the Statement of Work expressly states that it overrides a specific provision.

2.3 The Company is not obliged to order any minimum volume of Services. The Supplier shall not start work under a Statement of Work until it has been signed by both parties.

2.4 The Supplier shall provide the Services from locations within the European Economic Area, unless the Company has agreed otherwise in writing.

## 3. Supplier personnel

3.1 The Supplier shall appoint a relationship manager with authority to act on its behalf in all matters relating to this Agreement and shall notify the Company of any change in that appointment.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that key personnel named in a Statement of Work are not removed from the Services without the Company's prior written consent, except in cases of illness, resignation or termination of employment.

3.3 The Supplier shall ensure that its personnel comply with the Company's site rules, information security policies and code of conduct as notified to the Supplier from time to time.

3.4 The Company may require the Supplier to remove from the Services any individual whose conduct or performance the Company reasonably considers unsatisfactory, and the Supplier shall promptly provide a suitably qualified replacement at no additional cost.

3.5 The Supplier is solely responsible for the remuneration, taxes and social security contributions of its personnel and shall indemnify the Company against any claim that any of its personnel is an employee of the Company.

## 4. Company obligations

4.1 The Company shall provide the Supplier with timely access to its premises, systems, data and personnel as reasonably required for the Services and as specified in the relevant Statement of Work.

4.2 The Company shall appoint a contract manager who will be the Supplier's principal point of contact for the Services and who has authority to make decisions on the Company's behalf.

4.3 If the Supplier's performance is prevented or delayed by an act or omission of the Company, the Supplier shall notify the Company promptly and is entitled to a reasonable extension of time, provided that it has used reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effect of the delay.

## 5. Change control

5.1 Either party may request a change to the Services or to a Statement of Work by submitting a written change request describing the change and the reasons for it.

5.2 Within ten (10) Business Days of receiving or submitting a change request, the Supplier shall provide a change proposal setting out the impact on the Services, timetable, Charges and any other terms.

5.3 No change is effective until a change note has been signed by both parties. Until then, the Supplier shall continue to perform the Services as if the change had not been requested.

## 6. Acceptance

6.1 Each Deliverable shall be subject to acceptance testing against the acceptance criteria set out in the relevant Statement of Work.

6.2 The Company shall notify the Supplier within ten (10) Business Days of delivery whether a Deliverable passes or fails the acceptance tests. If a Deliverable fails, the Supplier shall remedy the defects and resubmit it within ten (10) Business Days.

6.3 If a Deliverable fails acceptance for a second time, the Company may reject it and recover any Charges paid for it, or accept it subject to a reasonable reduction in the Charges.

## 7. Charges and payment

7.1 In consideration of the Services the Company shall pay the Charges set out in Schedule 2 and in each Statement of Work. The Charges are exclusive of VAT, which the Company shall pay in addition on receipt of a valid VAT invoice.

7.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Company monthly in arrears for Services performed in the previous month, unless a Statement of Work provides for milestone payments. Each invoice shall include the purchase order number and a breakdown of time and expenses.

7.3 The Company shall pay each valid and undisputed invoice within the period set out in Schedule 2.

7.4 If the Company disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier within ten (10) Business Days of receipt and pay the undisputed part. The parties shall resolve the dispute in accordance with the dispute resolution clause.

7.5 Expenses are payable only if they are approved in writing by the Company in advance and comply with the Company's travel and expenses policy.

## 8. Service levels

8.1 The Supplier shall meet or exceed the Service Levels. If the Supplier fails to meet a Service Level, it shall promptly investigate the cause, report it to the Company and implement a remediation plan.

8.2 Service credits set out in Schedule 3 are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are without prejudice to the Company's other rights and remedies.

8.3 If the Supplier fails to meet the same critical Service Level in three (3) consecutive months, the Company may terminate the affected Statement of Work with immediate effect by written notice.

9.6 The obligations of confidentiality in this clause continue for a period of five (5) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement, and indefinitely in respect of trade secrets.

## 12. Information security

12.1 The Supplier shall maintain an information security management system certified to ISO/IEC 27001, or an equivalent standard approved in writing by the Company, for the duration of this Agreement.

12.5 The Supplier shall maintain role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and logs of access to Company Data that are retained for at least twelve (12) months and made available to the Company on request.

## 20. Step-in rights

20.1 If the Supplier fails to meet a critical Service Level for two (2) consecutive months, suffers a Security Incident affecting Company Data, or is subject to a force majeure event lasting more than ten (10) Business Days, the Company may take control of the affected Services itself or through a third party.

20.3 During step-in the Supplier shall cooperate fully with the Company and its nominee, provide access to the relevant systems, data and personnel, and continue to perform any Services not affected by the step-in.

20.4 The Supplier shall bear the reasonable costs of step-in where it results from the Supplier's breach, and the Company shall not pay the Charges for the affected Services for the duration of the step-in.

## 21. Business continuity and disaster recovery

21.1 The Supplier shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan that enables it to continue providing the services, or to restore them within the recovery time objective of four (4) hours and the recovery point objective of one (1) hour, following a disruptive event.

21.2 The Supplier shall test the plan at least once in each calendar year, including a full failover test of production systems, and shall provide the Company with a written summary of the test results, any gaps identified and the actions taken to close them.

21.3 The Supplier shall invoke the plan promptly on the occurrence of a disruptive event and shall keep the Company informed of progress at intervals of no more than four (4) hours until the services are fully restored.

21.4 The existence of a force majeure event does not relieve the Supplier of its obligation to implement the plan, and the Supplier may rely on force majeure only to the extent that a properly implemented plan would not have prevented or mitigated the effect of the event.

# Schedule 1: Services description

The Services consist of the workstreams listed below. Detailed scope, milestones and acceptance criteria for each workstream are agreed in the relevant Statement of Work.

| Ref | Workstream | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WS-01 | Data platform discovery | Inventory of existing data sources, pipelines and consumers; target architecture options with costs and risks |
| WS-02 | Ingestion framework | Reusable ingestion templates for batch and streaming sources with schema validation and dead-letter handling |
| WS-03 | Warehouse modelling | Dimensional model for sales, finance and operations domains with documented conformed dimensions |
| WS-04 | Data quality | Automated quality rules, anomaly detection and a quality dashboard with ownership per dataset |
| WS-05 | Metadata and lineage | Catalogue integration, column-level lineage capture and business glossary alignment |
| WS-06 | Access management | Role-based access to datasets aligned with the Company's identity provider and approval workflow |
| WS-07 | Reporting migration | Migration of 140 legacy reports to the new semantic layer with reconciliation evidence |
| WS-08 | Machine learning platform | Feature store, experiment tracking and model deployment pipeline for the analytics team |
| WS-09 | Cost management | Tagging standards, cost dashboards and monthly optimisation recommendations for cloud resources |
| WS-10 | Observability | Pipeline monitoring, alerting runbooks and on-call handover to the Company's operations team |
| WS-11 | Security hardening | Network segmentation, secrets management and vulnerability remediation for platform components |
| WS-12 | Knowledge transfer | Training curriculum, recorded sessions and pairing with Company engineers before handover |

1.1 The Supplier shall run each workstream using two-week iterations with a demonstration to the Company's product owner at the end of each iteration.

1.2 The Supplier shall maintain a single backlog for all workstreams, prioritised jointly with the Company, and shall publish a weekly status report covering progress, risks, issues and forecast effort.

1.3 All code produced under the Services shall be stored in the Company's source control system, reviewed by at least one other engineer before merging, and accompanied by automated tests with at least eighty per cent line coverage for new modules.

1.4 The Supplier shall document architecture decisions in the Company's decision register and keep operational runbooks up to date for every component it builds or modifies.

1.5 The Supplier shall provide second-line support for components it has delivered during business hours (08:00 to 18:00 Warsaw time) for the duration of the relevant Statement of Work.
